# Clueffy

**One word. Follow the clues.**

Clueffy is a free, independent browser word game. Each puzzle has one six-letter English answer, five guesses and five clues. You start with one clue — each valid wrong guess reveals the next one.

Play it here: https://clueffy.com

## How to play

1. Read the clue beside the board and think of a six-letter word it could describe.
2. Type on your keyboard or tap the on-screen letters, then press Enter.
3. A wrong-length word, an unrecognized word or a duplicate guess does not use a chance.
4. Read the letter colours and the newly unlocked clue before your next guess.
5. Solve within five guesses. A fifth valid wrong guess ends the round and shows the answer.

## Letter colours

- **Green** — right letter, right position. Keep that position.
- **Orange** — the letter exists in another position. Try it in a different spot.
- **Dark** — that copy is not in the answer. Avoid it unless another copy was confirmed.

Exact-position matches are assigned first, then remaining copies. So an orange E and a dark E in the same row can mean the answer contains only one E, already assigned.

## Daily and practice

- The daily puzzle resets at **midnight UTC** and is the same for everyone on that UTC date.
- Practice mode draws from a rotating collection of **60 original puzzles**; words can recur. Practice does not affect your daily record or streak.
- Progress is saved in your browser only — no account, payment or download is needed.

## Hints and answers

Three optional hints, behind separate disclosures, offer progressively more help: a gentle nudge, a clearer connection, then the first letter.

Opening a hint during an unfinished daily round marks that round as **assisted**. Revealing the answer ends an unfinished daily round without a win. Looking at help after finishing a daily puzzle does not change that finished result.
