Clueffy

One word. Follow the clues.

A free, independent browser word game. Each puzzle has one six-letter answer, five guesses and five clues. A wrong guess reveals another clue, while coloured letters help you narrow down the word.

Play Clueffy free →

How to play

  1. Read the clue, guess a six-letter word.
  2. Green = right letter, right spot. Orange = letter belongs elsewhere. Dark = not in the word.
  3. Solve within five guesses — each valid wrong guess unlocks the next clue.

Daily puzzle resets at midnight UTC, shared worldwide. Practice mode offers 60 original puzzles and does not affect your daily streak. No account or download needed.

Hints, without spoiling more than you want

Three optional hints: a gentle nudge, a clearer connection, then the first letter. Opening a hint on an unfinished daily round marks it assisted; revealing the answer ends that round without a win.
